## Full description (max 4000)

Serious Sudoku is made for the sudokus where you cannot get further by simple
logic alone. Besides the usual pencil marks it lets you make a guess, keep
solving, and return to the point of guessing when the guess turns out to be wrong.
Guesses can be nested, so you can do a depth-first search by hand.

Features
- Seven levels from Beginner to Grandmaster, with thousands of sudokus
- Pencil marks, filled in automatically on request and removed when they become impossible
- Guessing: guess a digit, continue, and reject the guess with one tap
- Undo
- Highlighting of the row, column, block and equal digits of the selected cell
- Enter your own sudoku
- Mark mistakes or let the solver finish the sudoku
- Your solve times per level (personal history)
- Light and dark mode
- Works completely offline, no ads, no accounts, no tracking
- Your game is saved automatically

Open source: https://github.com/chbauman/serious-sudoku
